**Emojis** are standard Unicode pictographs built directly into modern operating systems. They are incredibly useful in documentation, project management, and digital gardens to create visual hierarchy and quickly identify the status or domain of a note.

---

## ⌨️ Global Keyboard Shortcuts

You do not need to copy and paste emojis from the web. Use these native OS shortcuts to bring up the universal emoji picker anywhere you can type text:

- **Windows:** `Win` + `.` (Period) OR `Win` + `;` (Semicolon)
- **Mac:** `Cmd` + `Ctrl` + `Space` (OR the `Globe/Fn` key on newer MacBooks)
- **Linux (Ubuntu/GNOME):** `Ctrl` + `.` (Period) or right-click -> "Insert Emoji"
- **Mobile:** Tap the `☺` or `🌐` icon on the virtual keyboard.

## 🗂️ Curated Developer Cheat Sheet

### 🤖 AI & Agentic Workflows
Useful for mapping out multi-agent architectures, LLM interactions, and tools.
* **Core:** 🤖 (Agent) | 🧠 (LLM/Reasoning) | ⚡ (Action/Execution)
* **Components:** 🛠️ (Tools/Functions) | 👁️ (Sensors/Perception) | 🦾 (Actuators)
* **Data Flow:** 📥 (Input/Prompt) | 📤 (Output/Response) | 🔄 (Loop/Orchestration)
* **Network:** 🌐 (Web Search) | 🔌 (Plugin/MCP) | 🧩 (Module)

### 💻 Infrastructure & Engineering
* **Hardware:** 💻 (Local) | 🖥️ (Server) | 📱 (Mobile)
* **Cloud & Data:** ☁️ (Cloud) | 🗄️ (Database) | 📦 (Container/Docker)
* **Code:** 👨‍💻 (Dev) | ⚙️ (Config/Settings) | 🐛 (Bug) | 🧪 (Testing)

### 🚦 Status & Priority Tracking
Perfect for Kanban boards, Dataview queries, or task lists.
* **Status:** ✅ (Done) | ⏳ (In Progress) | ⏸️ (Paused) | ❌ (Failed/Cancelled)
* **Priority:** 🔴 (P0/Critical) | 🟠 (P1/High) | 🟡 (P2/Med) | 🔵 (P3/Low)
* **Health:** 🟢 (Healthy/Up) | 🟡 (Degraded) | 🔴 (Down/Outage)

### 📝 Note-Taking & Documentation
* **Structure:** 📌 (Pinned/Important) | 🔗 (Link/Reference) | 📑 (Index/MOC)
* **Content Types:** 📖 (Reading) | 💡 (Idea/Concept) | ❓ (Question)
* **Actions:** ✍️ (Drafting) | 🔍 (Research) | 🧹 (Refactoring/Cleanup)

## 💡 Obsidian-Specific Pro-Tips

### 1. File & Folder Naming
Adding emojis to the beginning of your file or folder names makes them stand out in the file explorer.
* *Example Folders:* `📂 01 - Projects`, `🧠 02 - AI Concepts`, `🛡️ 03 - Compliance`
* *Note:* Obsidian handles Unicode perfectly, so this will not break your internal `[[wikilinks]]`.

### 2. Customizing Callouts
You can override the default icon of any Obsidian Callout block by adding an emoji right after the type identifier:

> [!note|⚙️] Architectural Decision
> We are using `gemini-2.5-flash` for the orchestration routing to reduce latency.
