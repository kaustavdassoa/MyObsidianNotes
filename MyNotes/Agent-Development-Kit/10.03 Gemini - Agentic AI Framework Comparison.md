### 👉 Architect’s Recommendation Framework

Do not pick a framework based on GitHub stars. Make your decision using this sequential logic tree:

**Step 1: Identify your existing infrastructure constraints.**

- Are you an **Azure / .NET** shop with strict compliance needs? Stop looking. Use **Semantic Kernel**.
    
- Are you a **Google Cloud** shop requiring containerized scale, multi-language support (Go, Java, TS, Python), and deep data integration (BigQuery, Looker)? Use **Google ADK**.
    
- Are you strictly tied to **OpenAI** and want the absolute lowest friction? Use **OpenAI Agents SDK**.
    

**Step 2: Define your tolerance for "Autonomy" vs "Determinism".**

- If a failure costs you money or reputation, you need _Explicit Control Flow_. Choose **LangGraph** or **Google ADK**. They allow you to define strict graphs, trap errors, and enforce human-in-the-loop checkpoints before execution.
    
- If you are building an internal research tool where "trying different paths" is a feature, use **AutoGen** or **CrewAI**.
    

**Step 3: Evaluate your developer talent pool.**

- If your team consists of Data Scientists and AI Scripters who want to move fast without learning graph theory: Use **CrewAI**.
    
- If your team consists of Hardcore Backend Engineers who understand state machines, distributed systems, and concurrency: Use **LangGraph** or **Google ADK**.
    

**The Final Verdict:** For true production systems in 2026, the industry is converging on **State-Machine Orchestrators** (LangGraph) and **Cloud-Native SDKs** (Google ADK). Avoid general-purpose wrappers (LangChain) for agentic routing, and treat highly autonomous role-playing frameworks (CrewAI, AutoGen) as internal productivity tools rather than customer-facing architecture.
