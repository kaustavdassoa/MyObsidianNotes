> [!summary] Summary
> Evaluation of three LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) tasked with generating a production-grade architectural comparison of Agentic AI frameworks. **Claude emerged as the overall winner (8.9/10)** by prioritizing deep, decision-grade technical accuracy over strict table-formatting constraints.

---

## Core Concepts & Model Performance

> [!info] The Objective
> Generate a Gartner-level, 14-dimension comparison of AI frameworks (LangGraph, LangChain, CrewAI, Google ADK, AutoGen, Semantic Kernel, OpenAI Agents SDK) strictly inside a **single Markdown table**.

* **Model A (ChatGPT) - Score: 7.1/10**
    * **Strengths:** Covered baseline frameworks and provided a logical decision tree.
    * **Failures:** Skipped the mandatory "Comparison Summary" dimension and broke the single-table rule. Provided surface-level insights rather than brutal, architect-level precision.
* **Model B (Claude) - Score: 8.9/10 (Winner)**
    * **Strengths:** Delivered unparalleled factual depth and hit every prompt constraint flawlessly, identifying the modern state of the ecosystem (v0.9+ for CrewAI, etc.).
    * **Failures:** Heavily violated the single-table formatting constraint by generating 14 separate tables. Won because its architectural value outweighed the formatting flaw.
* **Model C (Gemini) - Score: 8.7/10**
    * **Strengths:** Successfully generated a single master table using HTML `<br>` tags and adopted the requested brutally precise persona.
    * **Failures:** Combined dimensions 11/12 and entirely omitted dimension 14 to force the data to fit the table constraints.
